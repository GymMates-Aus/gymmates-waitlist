Hey there,

You're on the GymMates waitlist. Thanks for trusting a thing that doesn't exist yet.

Quick story on why this exists. We run a gym in regional Victoria. We watch the same thing happen every year. New member signs up, comes in for three weeks, then quietly stops. It's almost never about the program. It's almost always about not having a mate there.

A peer-reviewed field experiment found that gym visits go up around 35% when people train with a friend instead of alone. We've seen it in our own gym for 13+ years. So we're building the thing we wished existed: an app that pairs you with one compatible mate at your gym, so showing up gets a little easier.

Here's what we'll send you, and only this:

- A note when your gym signs on, so you can be matched first.
- Once a month, a short update from the build. Wins, misses, what we changed because someone like you said "this bit's wrong."
- Early access ahead of public launch.
- A founding-member badge in the app. First-match priority on launch day.

That's the lot. No daily emails. No upsells. No selling your address.

If you want to help us go faster, the most useful thing you can do is tell one mate. Every signup tells us where to take GymMates next, and which gyms to chase first.

[**Tell a mate →**](https://waitlist.gymmates.com.au/?ref={{referral_code}})

Talk soon.

Jack
Built in regional Victoria.

## Notes for setup

- **Personalisation tokens** — Beehiiv's referral code merge tag is `{{referral_code}}` (or your subscriber's link will appear via the referral programme block). Swap if Beehiiv's docs name it differently for your plan.
- **Subject A/B test ideas** for after launch (one-off, not for the welcome): "What we got wrong this month", "Two gyms signed on this week."
- **Tone audit** — no banned words (no "journey", "elevate", "transform", "platform", "solution"). No em dashes. Australian English. Audience-language defaults to "exercise" implicitly (the email is mixed audience).
